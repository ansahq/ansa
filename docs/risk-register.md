# Risk register — accessibility acceptance and framework gate

Last updated: 2026-11-18. **No risk in this register is closed.** "Blocking" means the stated
condition prevents F08 closure or the named release claim. Severity is about user impact if the
risk materialises, not exploitability.

The F10 decision in [ADR 0004](adr/0004-framework-gate-decision.md) narrows current support to a
**Windows 11 build 26200 x86_64 keyboard-first development foundation** on the pinned
`gpui 0.2.2`. It does **not** close F08 or the release gate, promote the candidate, or permit an
accessibility, IME, scaling, cross-platform or performance claim. The gate remains open as
described in [framework-gate.md](framework-gate.md).

Evidence base: [ANS-15 acceptance record](evidence/ans-15-accessibility-acceptance.md),
[ANS-13 gate record](evidence/ans-13-framework-gate.md),
[screen-reader protocol](accessibility/screen-reader-acceptance.md), and the dated observations
in `experiments/gpui-accessibility/`. Automated UIA checks are supporting evidence only; they are
not screen-reader journeys.

Owner roles are roles, not individuals. A role must be assigned to a person in the ticket before
the next verification action starts.

## Register rules

- New semantic gaps get a new ID; do not reuse or renumber.
- A risk may move to "closed" only with a real AT artefact linked from the ticket and a second
  tester's agreement. Automated checks never close a risk.
- A11Y-01/02 are re-evaluated only by the framework gate decision (ANS-13 owns that record;
  the current decision is [ADR 0004](adr/0004-framework-gate-decision.md)).
- GATE-01…GATE-06 are gate/process risks. They may close only with the evidence named in their
  next-verification cell and a matching update to ADR 0004; they must not be renumbered into
  A11Y rows.
- This file does not change the framework gate or implementation status; it references them.
